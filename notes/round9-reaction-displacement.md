# Reaction displacement (0808170C)

Tags: #matching #scene-actors

The guarded displacement callback reaches 87.7% against the reviewed 1,168-byte
extent. It uses the same signed quarter-step as the Vorn, Skeleton, Mummy,
Bat and Mimic recovery handlers, retaining pointers to the two coordinates
until their clamped updates. The route gate, control timers and displayed
sequence now use their shared field definitions.

The draft is eight bytes long. The main differences begin in the prepared
flag reset and include the separate byte and word zeros kept by the ROM.
