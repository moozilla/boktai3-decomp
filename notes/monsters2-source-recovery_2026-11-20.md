# Golem through Axe source recovery

Tags: #monsters #matching #compiler #phase3 #structs

The eight reviewed units went from 438 reported warnings at intake to 173
with zero errors. The linter changes also exposed three existing sites.
Every touched translation unit matched with stock agbcc. The coordinator
stopped the review during Axe, before Glove.

- Golem 080C9848 and Mummy 080D3878 use the shared defeat spark ring with
  the named random table. Ivy 080EE1B0 still reloads the address between
  rings in the named-table drafts.
- Golem 080C9C04 and Mummy 080D1784 use shared route operations. The Mummy
  recovery needs the ordinary route check and terrain snap in the same
  block to retain the ROM stack layout.
- The shared drawable operations have their own header and match across
  the affected actors. Sword 080F5B68 uses the existing wave-component
  layout and action transition. Axe 080F9BE4 and its trail and collider
  paths work with ordinary positions in place of union overlays.
- Linter tests cover discarded scalar arithmetic and comma-group
  constant locals. The remaining addresses, masks and phase clears are
  recorded in the worker report.
