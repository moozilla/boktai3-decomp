## Source changes worth reviewing

### Compiler-warning work

- **ScenePanel_TickRig, 0805AC3C:** direct schedule-field comparisons remove two
  fabricated dead arms, two volatile views, and a reused interval value. The
  stock 220-byte function remains exact.
- **AreaEffect_UpdateActionParticles, 0804E8D8:** real `SceneVec` position/size
  fields replace packed-halfword masks. This removes one genuine proved-zero
  report; all 352 bytes match.
- **Dog_UpdateCardinalTurn:** the private animation-selector duplicate is
  replaced by the already shared helper used by related monsters. Four reports
  disappear because the duplicate is gone. These were legitimate constant-flag
  specializations in a generic helper, not four fabricated gameplay branches.
  The complete Dog unit remains exact; no header move hides the reports.

### Constants, control flow and actual field types

The cumulative source changes remove **363 constant-local warnings**. The large
mechanical part removes 263 locals across 215 units with direct typed literals;
its full 735-candidate inventory is in
[round14-constant-removal-audit.tsv](round14-constant-removal-audit.tsv).
The animation and enemy passes remove a further 59 and 33 locals respectively.
The remaining eight source removals come from the initial task-state fixes,
scatter/Vanargand resets, modifier/board initialization and the typed effect
allocators.

The object/trigger/palette pass removes 16 statement expressions, three
single-pass wrappers and two address/dereference expressions. Actual indexed
modifier traversal replaces duplicate pointers. Normal nested conditions retain
short-circuit evaluation; direct expressions use the real region, position and
transition fields. No macro or helper was invented for this pass.

The address-expression pass goes beyond deleting `*(&x)`: typed pool iteration,
field-level flag merges, halfword random samples, signed preview IDs, direct
callback selection and real vector members explain 21 further removals. The
initial direct-deletion pass alone found only two of those. Octopus contributes
two more through ordinary complete-field flag values.

Two unrelated-value unions become independent locals in the pending-damage and
sprite-animation handlers. Four placeholder reports disappear through actual
alias/temporary removal, including two unused declarations; they are not merely
renamed. Other visible scaffolding in those units stays open.

## Detector changes — no source credit

**Rejected combine attempts: nine proved-zero false positives.** The diagnostic
previously reported transient zeros while the compiler explored transformations
it later rejected. Real serial, EEPROM and RTC bit reads demonstrate the defect.
Zero events are now emitted only when `try_combine` accepts the transformation.
There is no hardware or source-spelling exemption. Genuine masked/shifted zeros,
including volatile reads, remain detected. Full evidence:
[round14-accepted-zero-diagnostics.md](round14-accepted-zero-diagnostics.md).

**Control-flow keywords: 119 const-local false positives.** The declaration
pattern treated `goto` and `return` as type names. Excluding those two reserved
keywords removes 111 jump reports and eight return reports, while actual
constant declarations and arbitrary typedef names remain detected. Evidence:
[round14-constant-declaration-diagnostics.md](round14-constant-declaration-diagnostics.md).

Each correction has its own commit, regression tests, three demonstrated real
counterexamples and same-source before/after counts. **No warning kind was added
and no `concession-ok` comment was added.** No warning was silenced for a compiler
allocation rationale.

## What remains open

The three file-level volatile errors remain in TargetRegistry_RegisterActor
(0815F6A8), TargetRegistry_RegisterSceneActor (0815F6F0), and
SpriteTrail_UpdatePositions (081F2404). Ordinary shared-type/accessor/traversal
forms have been tested. The two registration functions lose a repeated global
load; the trail routine hoists its camera load. Private diagnostic experiments
isolate alias-base and loop-motion decisions, including agbcc's treatment of the
incoming r0 structure-return register as a private base. No changed compiler,
integer-handle disguise, invented global or replacement barrier was adopted.
The natural original source is still unresolved.

All original compiler-warning clusters were examined. Most fabricated guards
and zero expressions still remain: fragment-table PRE, animation-tail sharing,
particle velocity rereads, constant-table priorities, Skeleton/Vorn defeat
allocation, random-sample lifetimes, palette cursors and the RAM-base operand
order are documented. The useful ordinary drafts and the precise decisions are
in the field, input/camera and volatile-worker review notes linked below.

The other warnings are also unfinished. The broad constant pass leaves 472 of
its considered declarations open; its exclusions are not authenticity findings.
The animation/enemy passes preserve nonmatching direct substitutions and review
addressable fill words, aggregate fields and dynamic values separately. The
direct union split review leaves 14 of its 16 candidates unresolved. Many
remaining once-loops and volatile views still need source reconstruction.
The linter also misses some reused locals, declaration-order choices and
register-only dead arms; a lower total does not erase that debt.

Further statement-expression, address-expression and placeholder work is active
after this frozen checkpoint. It is not included in these counts or this bundle.
