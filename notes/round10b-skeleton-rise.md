# Skeleton_UpdateRiseRecovery (080BF0E4)

Tags: #matching #skeleton #global-alloc #pre #stack-slots

All 3,040 bytes match with stock agbcc. The category selects directional rise
animation 0x63, 0x65 or 0x67. On completion the actor resumes its route or
tries a new route toward the tracked cell. The function name is provisional.

The three rise cases need distinct mirror and variant bytes on the stack.
They share the selected-index and restart pointers. The final case computes
its own quadrant. Capturing the profile flags, base frame and facing variant
together preserves the register choices in both drawable paths. The matched
directional animation function at 080764C8 also groups its profile reads.

Once the actor and cell occupied r6 and r5, respectively, only the order of
cached field addresses remained. The ROM puts the category address at
SP+0x24 ahead of the four turn fields, and the cooldown address at SP+0x54.
Field accessors, the sequence and tile helpers, and the existing terrain and
control helpers give GCC the pseudo-register numbers and PRE hash-table size
for that order. The final ordinary compile matches all branch targets, literal
pools and stack displacements. No register pins or assembly barriers are used.
