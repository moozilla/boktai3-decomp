## The ROM

Each arm of `if (b->sprite.flags & 4)` loads `ldr r3, =0x0403B714` first, then
inlines the random read into angle (r4). Speed is in r5. Later, r4 is `&pos`, r5 is
the hoisted 0x3FF mask and r6 is the table. The origin is a dead constant. Its load
survives because combine folds its only use and leaves a `(use)` insn.

* `register u32 setup asm("r3")` becomes a plain `u32 setup;`.
* Only the v->y read keeps `ParticleRandom_ReadRelativeSample(setup, gRandomTable)`.
* The three position reads and the StartAnimation read become
  `ParticleRandom_ReadDirectSample(gRandomTable)`.

In this draft setup takes r5, angle r3 and speed r4.

What-if knobs on this draft:

* `AGBCC_PRI=setup:+2920` (and above) gives **MATCH**.
* +2000 leaves 16 differences.
* +1000 or less leaves 20.

So the only blocker is global-alloc order. Setup (3 refs) must be allocated before
angle, whose priority is 3000 (6 refs, live 40). Setup's natural priority is 85: live
88, doubled twice by REG_EQUIV (fact 231). Even with no doubling it would be 340. With
3 refs, no natural length gets it near 3000.

The same knob also matches when setup is kept in the v->y read plus any of the
position reads (w_01 and w_0123). With setup kept in the StartAnimation read, it
crosses calls and cannot take r3.
