## Open work and unsuccessful attempts

The two target-registration functions, 0815F6A8 and 0815F6F0, still declare the
singleton pointer volatile. Removing the qualifier loses the second singleton
load after the node's next-link store. Ordinary named fields, a typed link
destination, a link accessor/setter, and the existing generic-pointer parameter
interface do not recover that load. A diagnostic-only alias override matches
0815F6A8, confirming memory dependence as the blocking decision. These
experiments have not been substituted into the source.

081F2404 still declares the camera pointer volatile. Plain shared camera types
allow the pointer to be hoisted outside the sprite-history loop. Disabling that
one hoist diagnostically matches the original 304 bytes; scalar-component
projection and the existing shared layouts are being investigated. No compiler
override is part of a delivered source or build.

Private alias traces additionally identify agbcc's unconditional treatment of
incoming r0 as a structure-return base. That can incorrectly make a parameter's
stores appear disjoint from globals. The remaining task is to recover plausible
source that produces the observed reads, not merely to erase the warning.

All 19 dead-code and 45 proved-zero sites remain in this checkpoint. Their
remediation is in progress in isolated worker checkouts. All other warning
categories remain open as shown above. The dynamic `FRUIT_SLOT` and
`UNLOCK_SLOT` offset macros also remain visible debt: the current broad
`GameState` arrays do not yet reproduce their list induction when indexed
directly. They were not renamed, hidden, or newly introduced.
