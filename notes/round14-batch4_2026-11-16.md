## Three proved-zero reports are diagnostic false positives

The 35 proved-zero sites remain in the reported total. A new independent audit
establishes that three of them report backend-created zero intermediates inside
live source operations:

| Reported site | Actual live operation | Why the detector reports zero |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hresvelgr_UpdateTurningCharge, `0819FC34` | `(RandomValue() >> 3) % 2` selects the turn | Signed remainder lowering creates a sign correction that is zero for every unsigned halfword input. |
| Vanargand_UpdateAlternatingPairAttack, `081A9DDC` | The same expression selects an attack half | The same synthetic sign correction is folded. |
| Vorn_BeginPursuitRoute | Set flag 4, then clear flag 2 | Halfword-field lowering leaves a synthetic zero-valued preservation arm inside the live flag update. |

For the two random expressions, input 8 yields remainder 1. Exhaustive
halfword enumeration gives 32,768 zero and 32,768 one results; the actual table
initializer produces 512 of each among its 1,024 entries. Vorn input zero
becomes four and stays four. The real source operations survive compilation.

There are three warning sites and five raw diagnostic records. The evidence
traces accepted compiler transformations, including the exact intermediate
expressions; it does not infer a false positive merely from source spelling.
A narrow detector correction still needs operation provenance or an equivalently
specific proved-RTL rule. No broad syntax filter, production compiler change or
ignore was adopted. Genuine source-zero counterexamples remain detectable.

This finding does not authenticate the surrounding private helpers or Vorn
view. Natural unsigned RNG-helper alternatives do not match. An ordinary signed
Vorn member matches but preserves both the private view and the same report;
that cosmetic-only candidate was withheld.
