Lessons from 08088CD4:
* The burst's `20` and `2` arguments are CSE'd constants kept in r8/sb while 4, 1,
  1023 and 3 are rematerialised. Locals assigned at the first call
  (`duration = 20, scale = 2`) reproduce that; the table needed a pin.
* The random index is `index *= 2; v = *(u16 *)((u32)table + index); v &= 3;` for
  `ldrh r1; movs r2,#3; ands r1,r2`.
* `masked = value & mask2` (value read into its own local) gave
  `movs r1,#mask; ldrh r0; ands r0,r1`.
