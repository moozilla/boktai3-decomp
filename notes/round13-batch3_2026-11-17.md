## Compiler evidence

Vorn illustrates why assembly alone can misidentify a CSE problem. The original
and unwrapped final-state code both share the constant one. The private wrappers
raise its reference weight, assign it to r4, and cause reload to rematerialize it.
Without the wrappers it can stay in r5/r6. Reusing the function's completion-state
scalar across the three final calls recovers the required behavior. The remaining
spark values need extra definitions: after their first call, independent masked
zeros self-assign kind, spread and origin. Their references become 9, 11 and 9.
Using the same zero expression for all three lets CSE share it and leaves six
real instructions; distinct masks allow combine to erase each complete identity.
The exact persistent node is `d5e41c06b4d58a439dfaa74a41418c7bb5b3803d0992e2a03c42d583d6f6d26f`
in `build/matchsearch/Vorn_UpdateDefeat`.

Animated creation and tentacle retraction need opposite late sharing decisions.
The former retains distinct post-call USE counts in the animation arms, preventing
cross-jumping. The latter supplies equal USE tails to all four Advance paths,
preventing flow2 from creating a unique fallthrough USE and allowing cross-jumping.
Neither form emits an additional instruction in the final output.

The burst emitter's first four invariant moves reduce the first loop pass's
movement threshold from 13 to 1. Its random-index address is deferred to pass two,
after the reverse-counter initializer. The final source uses symbolic resource
addresses; the union staging disappears in combine. Facing motion's final copy
was confirmed by forcing the quotient to r1 diagnostically; sequential compound
shifts then obtained the same result with the stock compiler.

heading priorities tie at 12,000 after the lifetime change; declaration order
