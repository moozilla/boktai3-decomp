## Generated names for scene procedures

Tags: #history #scripts #tooling

`tools/assets/gclroles.py` names every scene entry by its area
(`white_forest_3880`) and 3,176 of the 3,930 unnamed scene-private
procedures by role (`_stage`, `_doors`, `_enemies`, `_objects`, `_music`,
`_cutscene_N`, `_after_cutscene_N`, `_on_arrival`, `_exit_to_SCENE`), from
how they are referred to and what they call. `gclview.Names` adds them at
confidence `auto` under `gcl/names/proc.csv`; the Studio's Scripts page
titles scene files by them and links procedures passed as values. See
[gcl/README.md](../../gcl/README.md#generated-names-for-scene-procedures).
